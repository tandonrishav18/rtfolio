Booking a ride should be simple. But what happens when one captain after another rejects your request?

​

For many commuters, finding a ride can mean repeated cancellations, uncertain waiting times, and the frustration of trying again.

​

Meanwhile, people travelling along the same roads often have empty seats in their vehicles.

​

The problem isn't always a lack of vehicles. Sometimes, it's a lack of connection between people going the same way.

Two different commuters. One shared need.

​

To explore the opportunity for community-powered ride sharing, we focused on two sides of the commuting experience: people struggling to find reliable rides and people already travelling with unused vehicle capacity.

​

While their motivations differ, both groups could benefit from a simpler way to connect along overlapping routes.

What if every journey could become a shared opportunity?

​

Introducing Co-Ride - a new way to move together.

​

Co-Ride is a community-powered ride-sharing concept designed within the existing Rapido ecosystem.

​

It connects commuters travelling along similar routes, allowing users to either volunteer their ride or join someone already heading their way.

​

Instead of creating another booking experience, Co-Ride builds on Rapido's familiar interface to make ride sharing simple, accessible, and convenient.

​

The idea is simple: You don't need another vehicle. You just need someone going your way.

Two different journeys. One shared destination.

​

Co-Ride connects Volunteers and Joiners through Rapido's familiar booking experience, making shared commuting simple and accessible.

​

  • Verified to ride. Ready to share.
  • ​

    • Volunteers can create their profiles, upload driving and vehicle documents, and verify them through DigiLocker. Once verified, they can register their vehicle and start offering shared rides.

    ​

    Key Aspect : Build trust through document and vehicle verification while keeping onboarding simple within Rapido's existing interface.

    ​

    *Women-Only Rides : Co-Ride also offers a women-only ride preference, allowing female commuters to connect and share journeys with other women for added comfort and safety.

    Already on your way? Make the journey count.

    ​

    Co-Ride allows verified volunteers to share their everyday commute with people travelling along similar routes.

    ​

    Users can select their destination, choose a registered vehicle, set available seats, and publish their ride. Once a joiner is accepted, OTP verification confirms the passenger before the journey begins.

    ​

    Key Aspect : Make volunteering effortless by integrating it into Rapido's existing booking flow, without requiring users to plan a separate trip.

    No captain available? Someone's already going your way.

    ​

    When a regular Rapido booking remains unmatched, Co-Ride offers an alternative : join a verified volunteer travelling along a similar route.

    ​

    Using the existing pickup and destination, users can find nearby volunteers, review ride details, and accept a shared journey without starting their search again.

    ​

    Key Aspect : Introduce Co-Ride at the moment users need an alternative, making ride discovery effortless without disrupting the familiar booking flow.

    Shared rides. Shared value. Smarter business.

    ​

    Co-Ride doesn't just connect commuters. It introduces a new revenue opportunity for Rapido by making use of journeys already happening.

    ​

    Through a small platform fee on completed shared rides, Rapido could earn revenue while volunteers receive compensation for sharing their available seats.

    ​

    By leveraging Rapido's existing user base, booking experience, and payment ecosystem, Co-Ride creates an additional revenue stream while making everyday commuting more affordable and accessible.

    Because sometimes, the ride you need is already on its way.
